# Week 01 — What Are You Proving?

## 1. Proof Statement

I can **build practical machine learning solutions using Python** for a **startup founder or technical lead looking for a junior AI/ML developer**, and I want them to **contact me about a project or opportunity**. My portfolio will demonstrate this through real projects, working implementations, and clear explanations of how I approach machine learning problems.

## 2. Why This Needs to Exist

My CV and LinkedIn can list my AI/ML skills, but a portfolio can **show actual evidence of how I use those skills to build and solve practical problems**.
